# Artwork style and character reference
Warm watercolour and coloured-pencil storybook illustrations, cream paper, indigo, leaf green, mango yellow and clay accents. Respectful everyday Nigerian settings; no invented landmarks, logos or image lettering. Decorative art never supplies assessed quantities.

Amina: dark skin, round face, mustard headscarf, teal dress. Musa: dark skin, cropped hair, blue shirt, brown trousers. Zainab: dark skin, two short braids, coral blouse, navy skirt. Daniel: dark skin, short curls, green shirt, grey trousers; wheelchair when seated. These fictional characters represent varied children, not all Gombe clothing or communities.

Reference sheet: separate full-body portraits in that order on a plain pale background. Scenes: airy classroom with bottle tops; shaded produce stall with supervising adult; school garden measuring with string and containers. Keep faces and clothing consistent.

Exact dots, shapes, rulers, clocks and grids are deterministic SVG. No generated currency images. Photography requires location, photographer, source and verified reusable permission. The unchanged Kilang Hill photograph is included under its verified CC BY-SA 4.0 licence and source caption. Further photographs require the same provenance checks.
