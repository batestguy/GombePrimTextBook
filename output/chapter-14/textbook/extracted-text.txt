Guide, words and next learning
Read together. Use the workbook or an exercise book.
Length / Educational review edition
Prepare: similar safe paper strips, pencils, book, reachable table, fixed
paper-strip unit and label. Adult cuts paper. Clear a short route; supervise
steps. No ruler or centimetres are needed. Read aloud; pointing, speech and
moving objects are valid responses. Place all materials within reach.
Misconceptions and help: If a pupil chooses by position/colour, align starts
and trace to the far ends. If ordering is hard, compare two then add the third.
If units gap or overlap, mark each finish and restart there. Different people's
counts alone do not show longer: use one person's unit. For leftovers record
full units and a part. Recheck with new materials on the try-again page.
Glossary: length: extent end to end; longer/shorter: more/less length; equal length:
same reach with starts aligned; distance: how far between places; hand span:
thumb-tip to little-finger-tip spread; unit: one chosen repeat; step: one repeated
pace; order: arrange by length.
