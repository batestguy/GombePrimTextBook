TEACHER AND REVIEWER / KEEP WITH THIS CHAPTER
Guide and answers
Two introduction pages, four counting pages and this teacher page.
P1-01  |  Provisional chapter for review
7
Goal: Count, read, write and order 1 to 5.
Words: count; number; how many.
Materials: five large, clean counters and number cards 1 to 5. Use pieces too large to swallow,
with adult supervision.
Teach and notice
Read the introductions and story aloud; independent reading is not required. Let a pupil move
each counter into a counted group. Ask, "How many altogether?" Look for one touch per object
and use of the last number as the total. Vary spacing while keeping the count unchanged. Model
numeral formation before writing. Accept speech, pointing, movable cards or an adult scribe as
appropriate; check writing separately.
Answers and observation
1. 3
2. 5
3. Two separate circles.
4. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
5. 4
6. Touch or move each top exactly once.

unverified.
