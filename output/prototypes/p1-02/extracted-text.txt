TEACHER GUIDE
FOR THE TEACHER
Teach, notice and return
P1-02 / Sort, match and order / Mr Aliyu leads.
P1-02 / Chapter review edition
Objective. Sort collections and compare small sets. Also retain the source ordering
practice within 1 to 5. Read all pupil text aloud. Prerequisite: count objects to five; revisit
the Chapter 1 model as needed. The pupil pages have no numbered instructions.
Concrete start. At a reachable table, provide three clean, large leaves and five large,
clean tops. Use pieces too large to swallow; supervise use. These are pretend tickets and
counters, not food. Sort by type in front of pupils, then invite them to sort a separate tray. A
set means a collection. Reuse this same three-leaf/five-top tray for the ending. No pieces
are added or removed.
Model, practise, compare. Enact p. 2 with real pieces before using the pictures. On p. 3
pair two tops with four, one to one, and point to the two unmatched tops. Name more and
fewer. On the equal-row activity, invite a prediction, then match: all three have partners in
both rows, so the sets have the same number even when spaced differently. On p. 4 count
the dot sets 2, 3, 4 before connecting them to numerals. Read the numerals aloud.
Observe and help. Confuses type with colour: restate leaves together/tops together, sort
one, then ask the child to place another. Counts twice: move each counted object aside.
Chooses by row length: bring one object beside one partner. Gives a partner twice:
physically move paired objects together. Cannot order: build sets of 1, 3 and 4, then
compare adjacent sets. Recheck each difficulty with fresh small sets; accept speaking,
pointing or moving objects before writing.
Recall and fresh checks. On p. 6 wait before offering help. After support, change the
quantities (for example 1 and 2) and ask again. For this recheck, 2 has more and 1 has fewer.
Keep p. 7 fresh tasks independent before feedback. On another day, ask what Mr Aliyu showed.
Give four leaves and two tops to sort and compare, then ask pupils to order 5, 2, 3. Answers:
leaves/tops; four leaves more, two tops fewer; 2, 3, 5. Record independent / with help / not yet.

P1-02 / Chapter review edition
Every example and added task
p. 1 tray and p. 2 sort model: 3 leaves and 5 tops. Guided sort: the leaf in the second position is
already circled; point to the leaf in the fourth position. Tops are first and third. Grace moves her
leaf to the leaves.
p. 3 model: 2 matched pairs, 2 unpaired tops in the four-set; 4 more, 2 fewer. Guided rows: 3 in
each, every top has a partner; same number. Any one-to-one bijection is valid.
p. 4 model: 2, 3, 4 dots in increasing order.
p. 6 support: 2 pairs and 1 unmatched lower dot; lower set of 3 has more. Accept either valid
line from the remaining upper dot to a free lower dot.
p. 7 resolution: 3 pairs, 2 spare tops; tops more, leaves fewer. Put those 2 unmatched tops in a
real spare box. Read the closing dialogue after doing this action.
p. 7 fresh sort: circle second and fourth objects (leaves). Fresh comparison: 5 leaves more than
4 tops; circle the right set.
