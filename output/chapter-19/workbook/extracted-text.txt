Collect your own new records
Read together. Speak, point or write here.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Keep completed records and help pages covered. Work without answer help.
An adult sets up safe resources and may read these words neutrally.
AGE: choose two new person letters. Ask permission privately. Ask EACH willing
person, "How old are you in completed years?" Listen, record each letter and reply in
years, then read it back to check. If someone declines, an adult plays TWO different
fictional people and gives each reply only after you ask. Do not copy printed replies.
HEIGHT: use TWO new adult-prepared full-size paper stand-ins and TWO blank strips.
Label each strip to match its stand-in. Put its bottom at that stand-in's floor line.
Observe and mark the top of its head. Repeat with the SAME method. Align strip
bottoms; compare marks; record both labels and taller, shorter or equal. Positioning
help is allowed; the observation is yours.
Do not climb or touch a person. A seated pupil can observe and mark a reachable paper stand-in. No
prepared resources means height collection is not assessed practically. Real age replies stay private;
letters alone are not anonymity.
