P1-19-HEIGHT-MODEL / model
Mrs Binta uses adult-prepared full-size paper silhouettes D and E, with foot bottoms at

and record actual results. Fixed guided diagram F shorter than G, G taller than F is
only interpretation, not evidence of new collection. Actual ages and heights are
conditional; accept accurate different results and record support.

Prepare a reachable table, large smooth paper strips, safe removable head-marking
materials and full-size upright paper silhouettes with clear head tops and foot
baseline. Adult positions paper on a reachable surface; no stools, climbing, sharp pins
or touching pupils. Printed figures are small method diagrams, never life-size. A
seated pupil can mark stand-ins with positioning help. Do not require John or anyone
to stand; never compare sitting height with standing stature.
Use a consenting adult to role-play two different fictional people for each collection.
One can play a home role and another a school role. Choose new completed-year
ages within taught number range; reply only AFTER the child asks. Never present a
completed table as interviewing evidence. Real collection is private and voluntary:
accept decline, substitute fictional roles, do not display identifiable ages; letters alone
do not guarantee privacy.
Score separately: permission/consistent question; listening and accurate age
recording; labelled new head marks/common baseline; comparison and accurate
recording. Record fictional simulated versus actual willing-person collection, resource
positioning/reading/transcription help versus mathematical prompts. Accept all
accurate conditional ages and taller/shorter/equal results. No resources: practical
collection not assessed. Do not invent success. Copies of printed records assess
interpretation only.

bottoms, compare. Recall before help; new unsupported recheck; new fresh and scheduled delayed
