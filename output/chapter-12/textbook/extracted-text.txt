Teaching across short sessions
Adult guidance / Read pupil prompts aloud.
Chapter twelve / Educational review edition
Teach one new missing-number position at a time. Revisit addition and
subtraction with counters before this chapter. Read all prompts aloud.
Introduce - as take away and + as join; = means equal amounts. A
blank represents a number, not an instruction to calculate everything on
its left.
Suggested sequence: equality and addition; removed and starting
amounts; reversed equality and substitution; related families; everyday
checking; independent practice; targeted help and recheck; story
ending and fresh checks. Take additional short sessions when needed.
This is editorial pacing, not an official scheme.
Textbook/workbook page pairs: 12/1, 13/2, 14/3, 15/4, 16/5; help 17/6
through 22/11; fresh checks 24/12, 25/13, 26/14. Workbook page labels are
printed above each title. Textbook users can use counters and an ordinary
exercise book for every response. Do not mark the textbook.
Keep counters large and reachable. Accept speech, pointing, moving
objects or drawing as evidence before writing. Never tell John an answer
before his turn. Rotate the helper and speaker. Count hidden story
bottle tops only after opening; the artwork does not encode their
number.

Notice, help and check later
Adult guidance / Read pupil prompts aloud.
Chapter twelve / Educational review edition
Look for: adding the visible numbers regardless of blank position;
treating = as an answer arrow; giving the removed amount when the
start is unknown; guessing without substitution; counting a counter
twice. Ask the pupil to name whole, known part and missing part before
moving objects.
Support: choose the matching smaller-step page. Model with counters
and allow the pupil to copy the action. Close that example, put those
counters away and use the new recheck. Record success by objective,
not by a total score. Re-teach if the recheck is unsuccessful.
Fresh check: use the last three pupil practice pages without help. All
six objectives have separate fresh evidence. At a later session, use the
six prompts in the complete answer map marked delayed. Keep the
book closed and use new objects. Record prompt, response, method
and any help.
Story preparation: place exactly 4 bottle tops in an opaque bag and 3
on the mat before the opening. Tell the whole 7 without displaying the
hidden set. Do not reveal the 4 until the story ending. All independent
tasks use separate sets; they do not alter the story stock.

Blank: a place for one missing number. Part: some of a whole. Whole:
all the parts together. Equal: the same amount. Substitute: replace
the blank with a number and check the original sentence. Related:
sentences using the same whole and its two parts.

everyday addition checks; P1-p06-03 missing subtraction; P1-p06-04
