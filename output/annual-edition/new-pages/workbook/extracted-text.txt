Every older reference to textbook/workbook page numbers means the LOCAL
SOURCE extract named in that strip, not the annual folio. The source-to-annual
directory below converts it. Textbook and workbook chapter titles match.
Old ‘opening’, ‘extract’ or ‘next annual assembly’ scope statements describe their
preserved source editions. This annual cover and navigation supersede that
packaging, while all teaching and answers are retained.

final AI teaching pages; adult guidance, answers, words, sources and Primary 2
preview.

printed pretend AI activities. No device, account, phone, live AI or internet is
required.

system output. An adult reads with pupils; everyone checks with their own maths.
