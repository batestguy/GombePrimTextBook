Suggested terms and checks are editorial pacing, not an official
scheme. Term one revisits Chapters 1–5; term two Chapters 6–12; term
three Chapters 13–19. Review stations sample earlier learning. Every
historical objective remains linked to its full chapter teaching and
checks in the objective ledger, not certified by these small mixed
checks.
Read instructions aloud without supplying a mathematical answer.
Record separately: oral explanation, written/numeral response, practical
action; unaided, after neutral rereading, or after teaching/support.
Record not assessed when resources or access are missing. Arrange
reachable safe materials; accept speaking, pointing and moving objects.
Teacher may explain in pupils’ familiar language; no translations are
asserted here.
After an error, identify the method: double counting, unaligned ends,
unequal parts, place-value confusion. Return to the matching chapter
model with smaller real sets. Recheck with different values after
support, then again after several days without the model. Record the
exact task, response, help and date. Do not infer mastery of untested
objectives or outcomes.
