Ready for bigger numbers

names. These are different checks.
Copy 12 here. Cover it and all earlier answers. Write the new number
your adult says in the lower space.

Twenty-one to twenty-five

Remember and repair
Tell first. Your adult can record your spoken explanation.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Explain how ten loose ones become a new ten.
Choose a number from 21 to 99. Build it. Draw and record tens and
ones. Say its name.
After help, try a NEW number your adult gives. Build it, say it and write
it without copying.

CHAPTER SIX / NUMBERS TO NINETY-NINE
A fresh number try
An adult prepares new materials using the textbook adult list.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Count the real set your adult gives. Record the total.
Build the two numbers your adult says. Draw bundles and ones.
Read each shuffled card aloud. Then point to the cards your adult
names. Do not use pictures to guess the names.
Cover every card, model and ALL earlier handwritten answers. Write the
new spoken numbers below or on fresh paper.
