Prepare. Large safe strips, bands, reachable trays, three boxes, cards 1-99, paper and
pencils. A bundle is ten separate strips. Open and recount it. The binding is not counted.
Artwork is decorative; exact mathematics uses vector diagrams or actual strips.
Progression. Prior 1-20 check first. Re-teach any gaps before larger numbers. Use
several sessions for 21-25, then 26-29, then each decade through 99. For EVERY
number in a band, model build/name/formation, build/read/write together, then
independent samples. Do not present the band list as one rapid lesson. All numerals use
already-taught digits 0-9; demonstrate live formation and spacing again as needed.

ones. Ones: single items; ones place records loose ones. Tens place: left place in
these two-digit numerals. Zero: no loose ones in 30,40,...,90. Sequence: numbers in
an order. Bundle: tied ten, checked by opening and counting.
