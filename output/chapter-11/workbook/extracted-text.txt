Cross ten together
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
A line through a dot means it was taken away. Watch this completed
example: 4 dots, 1 crossed out, 3 left.
4 − 1 = 3. Check: 3 + 1 = 4.
Now read "Try crossing ten together" with an adult. Cross out 5 of these
12 dots: the 2 in the short row first, then 3 from the ten. Count the
uncrossed dots.
12 − 5 = ______
Draw two groups: left and taken away. Add them back to check. Explain
why 8 left would be wrong.

Help and a fresh try
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Tell what subtract means and how adding back checks. With an adult,
use 3 counters. Move 1 aside. Write 3 − 1 = 2 and its addition check
here.
Try 5 − 2 on your own. Write the answer and addition check.
For help across ten, let an adult show 11 − 2 with counters. Then start
again with 11 dots below. Cross out 3 on your own. Write 11 − 3 and an
addition check.
