Glossary: subtract (−): take away an amount. Remain/left: still there after taking
away. Zero (0): none. Check: see if an answer is right. Add (+): join amounts. Equals
(=): has the same value. Backwards: move towards smaller numbers, one step for
each object removed.

through eighteen including crossing ten; P1-p05-03 accuracy by adding back.
