Maths with Friends  •  Workbook
2
Read with an adult
Ask an adult to read the words and help you find a page. Take turns. Ask
questions. Everyone can join in.
Use your workbook
Write and draw in the spaces in this workbook. Use the textbook to see the
teaching and stories. An adult can help you find the matching lesson.
Find a lesson
Look at the contents on the next pages. The number beside a title tells you
the page in THIS workbook. An adult can help you read the page numbers.
Try in your own way
You can speak, point, move objects or draw. Use safe materials within easy
reach. Take time to explain your thinking.
