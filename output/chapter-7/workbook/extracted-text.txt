Fold, check and retry
Read together. Say, point or use your workbook or exercise book.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
For help, fold a scrap rectangle with an adult. Match the edges. Open
and check the equal halves. Fold a square twice in different directions.
Open and touch one quarter.
Now work alone with new scrap paper. Fold a tall rectangle into equal
halves. Fold a new square into equal quarters. Point to one part of each.
Say its name and explain how you checked.
Draw your new folds here, or keep your folded papers with this
workbook. An adult may record your explanation.
If ready, show a different way to make equal halves of a square on spare paper.

Our fair display
Read together. Say, point or use your workbook or exercise book.

Aishatu needs a paper mat with the same space for each friend. Divide
this whole square into four equal parts. Put one drawing or name in
each part: Aishatu, Adamu, Grace and John.
Say why each part is a quarter. Explain how the mat gives everyone the
same amount of space. An adult may record your words here.
