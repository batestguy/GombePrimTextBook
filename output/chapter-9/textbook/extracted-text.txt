Chapter nine / Educational review edition
Glossary: count on: keep a known amount and say the next numbers for added
objects. Sum/total: how many altogether. Addend (adult term): an amount being
added. Zero: no objects in the set. Check: use a method to see whether a result is
right. Equals (=): has the same value as.
