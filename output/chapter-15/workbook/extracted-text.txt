Remember, then get help
Read together. Listen while an adult reads the cards.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Adult: cover the help and new try below until recall is recorded.
Before help: close earlier pages. Explain first, next, last and
before/after. Tell a pretend home/school story. Say when each happens.
Now use help if needed: at home in the evening, open a book; read it;
close it. Try two cards first, then add the last. Which comes before
reading? After reading? At our pretend school, the teacher reads in the
afternoon.
New try without answers: at school in the afternoon, take clay, then
shape it. Show the toy at home in the evening. Tell order, before/after,
home/school and when. An adult may read, then waits.

PRIMARY ONE / WORKBOOK
OUR DAY IN ORDER
Remember another day
Read together. Listen while an adult reads the cards.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Next session, without looking back: invent different home and
school activities. Say a day part for each. Tell a three-event story.
Choose a middle event. What is before it? What is after it?
You may use a fictional routine. If your story crosses night, say next
morning. Speak, point, act or draw. An adult listens without giving the
answer.
