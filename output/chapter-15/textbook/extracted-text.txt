P1-15-SEQUENCE-MODEL / model
Amina: wake, wash, eat, school. First wake; wash next; school last; eating follows washing
and is before school. These are this fictional story's choices, not a universal routine.
P1-15-DRAW-MODEL / model
Completed drawings: first wall; next wall with roof; last wall with roof and door. Roof is after
wall and before door. This is a drawing story, not a required home routine.
P1-15-DAY-MODEL / model
Within the stated day: morning, afternoon, evening, night. In Mrs Binta's story: home wash
morning; school reading morning; home toy-making afternoon; home storytelling evening;
home sleep night. Reading may also occur at home or other times; next morning follows
night.

P1-15-GUIDED / guided
Stated story: first take paper at school in the morning; next draw at school in the morning;
last show drawing at home in the afternoon. Drawing follows taking paper and is before
showing. Grace's reversal is corrected using the story, not the page position.
P1-15-INDEPENDENT / independent
Stated story: first collect a leaf at home in the afternoon; next draw it at home in the
afternoon; last show the picture at school the next morning. Draw is after collect and before
show; both contexts and stated parts of day are required. Across-night sequence is
explicitly two days.
P1-15-RECALL / recall
Without help, recall first/next/last and before/after; give a coherent story with one home
and one school activity, naming plausible day parts. Pointing, speaking or acting is valid;
accept fictional routines. Record recall before providing help.

P1-15-HELP / help
With two cards: first open a storybook at home in the evening; next read it at home in the

P1-15-RECHECK / recheck
New recheck without supplied answers: first take clay at school in the afternoon; next shape
clay there in the afternoon; last show the toy at home in the evening. Taking is before
shaping; showing after shaping. Name school/afternoon and home/evening. Support may
read and move cards but cannot supply the response.

Aishatu's toy-school picture sequence is wall, roof, door. Put the story strip with it: make toy
at home in the afternoon, then share at school next morning. Opening problem resolved by

P1-15-FRESH / fresh
New personal or invented three-event day: coherent first/next/last and before/after
explanation; at least one home and one school activity with a plausible day part for each.
Must differ from all taught example activity sets; no fixed universal routine. Adult records
events/order/contexts/times and explanation, not drawing quality.
P1-15-DELAYED / delayed
Next session, without looking back: invent different home and school activities with
plausible day parts; choose a middle event and explain one event before and one after in a
coherent three-event story. If across days, name next morning. Assess recall independently;
activity/time alternatives accepted with coherent story.
P1-15-Q1 / independent
morning: within the stated day, morning precedes afternoon.

Teach, notice and recheck
Teacher guidance / provisional chapter extract
Time / Educational review edition
Use: Short sessions; read every card aloud, let pupils point, speak or move
reachable cards. No clock/calendar knowledge needed. A made-up routine is always
allowed. Do not ask for private home information. Drawing quality and handwriting
are not time understanding.
Notice: A child may sort by page position, confuse before/after, or think one activity
belongs to only one day part. Ask them to act the stated story. Reduce to two cards,
say earlier/later, then add a third. Use the book-opening help and new clay recheck
on textbook page 10/workbook page 5. Do not give the recheck answer.
Next session: Use workbook page 8, or an exercise book, for P1-15-DELAYED.
Require new activities and both contexts with coherent day parts, a middle event
and before/after. Record response route, independent/support status, events, order,
times and explanation. A practical opportunity is not observed mastery. Reteach
only the uncertain part.
Glossary: event: something that happens; before: earlier; after: later; first: start of this
story; next: following event; last: end of this story; morning/afternoon/evening/night: named
parts of a day in this order. Days repeat: next morning follows night.
