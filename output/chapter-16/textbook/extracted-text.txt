ADULT GUIDANCE
Teach safely and record honestly
Provisional chapter extract / independent review required
Heavy and light / Educational review edition
Before practical work: an adult approves light safe objects, checks the
balance empty and level, free to move, stable, reachable and suited to the
loads. Avoid sharp, breakable or choking hazards. No pupil lifts heavy loads. A
small smooth stone is a supervised story example, not an instruction to use
unsafe stones.
Observe: predict first; one object on each pan; release hands; wait for rest;
record lower/higher/level and the comparison. Check pan contact, friction or
an unbalanced empty apparatus before interpreting. Record observed results,
including equal and predictions that prove wrong. Never award practical
mastery from pictures alone. No working balance means practical objective
not assessed.
Access and assessment: seated pointing, reachable placement, read-aloud
and a scribe writing the pupil's own words may preserve independence.
Record person/date/materials/prediction/pan
observation/comparison/support. Teaching hints invalidate that attempt as
independent: reteach, then use a genuinely new pair without answer help.
Recall: record initial recall before help. Cover help for the new recheck. After two or
three school days use another new pair and ask why bigger need not mean heavier.
These are opportunities to gather evidence, not observations of mastery.

P1-16-INTERPRET
B is heavier than A; A is lighter than B because B pan is lower.
P1-16-RECALL
Adult checks empty pans level and move freely; predict, place one safe light object
on each pan, release, wait to settle, observe. Lower load is heavier; higher lighter;
level at rest means equal on a working balance.
P1-16-HELP
Point to the lower stone pan, then the higher leaf pan. Stone heavier, leaf lighter in
this model. Adult reteaches one object on each pan and waiting.
P1-16-RECHECK
Use the actual observation, not object names. Accept heavier/lighter/equal with
correct pan explanation; no functioning balance means practical comparison not
assessed.

Sources, language and next steps

Glossary: weight: the property compared here by the balance; heavier: the
lower load pan on a checked balance at rest; lighter: the higher load pan;
equal: neither load heavier; balance: apparatus with two pans; pan: its dish;
predict: say what you think before testing; observe: notice what actually
happens.
Mapping: P1-p10-03, historical 2012 Primary 1 p.10: compare familiar
objects using light and heavy. Source tasks P1-16-Q1 to Q6 retain original
wording and answers in canonical data. All six are enacted locally in both
artifacts. Q4 adds the required working-balance condition; Q5 allows any
actual result; Q6 uses a stated observed counterexample. There are no
assigned supplements.
