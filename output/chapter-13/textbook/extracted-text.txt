values through 25k; find change from payments through 10k; rub genuine
coin surfaces through brown and white paper. Pretend arithmetic does not
establish genuine-coin identification. All real-resource stages are conditional
opportunities, not recorded pupil outcomes.

Chapter thirteen / Educational review edition
Routes and sequence. Textbook 1 story/goal; 2 values; 3 genuine model/guided
to workbook 1; 4 rubbing to workbook 2; 5 totals; 6 change; 7-9 independent
originals to workbook 3-5; 10 recall BEFORE help; 11-14 help and new rechecks; 15
closure; 16 fresh to workbook 6-7. Every printed independent prompt and value is
local in both artifacts. Respond in the exact workbook page OR an ordinary exercise
book; genuine activities use verified materials and separate sheets. No textbook
marking.
Misconceptions and support. Three cards do not mean 3k: read and add labels
(page 13). Change is not the cost: count from cost up to payment and add to check
(page 14). A copied outline is not rubbing (page 12). Size does not prove
denomination (page 11). Keep genuine-resource outcomes separate from pretend
arithmetic. Permit pointing, speech, seated handling and an adult scribe; record
help honestly.
Historical/provisional mapping. P1-p08-01 real recognition: S008; P1-p08-02
totals: Q1, Q2, Q5, Q6 plus new tasks; P1-p08-03 change: Q3, Q4; P1-p08-04
rubbing: S009. Q5 alone does not assess genuine rubbing. Baseline:
PRIMARY-1-TEACHING-MAP and PRIMARY-1-ASSUMPTIONS P1-A06. Current NERDC
alignment, adoption, local educator review, pupil/accessibility trials and
physical/printer approval remain pending.
