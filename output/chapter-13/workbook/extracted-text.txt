Read genuine coin values
Read together. Write or draw in the spaces.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Only begin when the adult has verified genuine examples and a
dated value checklist. Otherwise the adult writes not assessed.
Look at each example. Point to it and say its value by yourself.
Point to a pretend card and say how it differs.
Adult record: examples, source and date; pupil's exact values; correct / needs
help / not assessed. Do not guess values or draw substitute coins.

Try a real coin rubbing
Read together. Write or draw in the spaces.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Only begin with a verified genuine coin, brown paper, white paper
and a pencil. An adult supervises. Put the coin under brown paper.
Hold the paper still. Gently rub with the pencil side on the paper.
Repeat with white paper. Say the coin value.
Keep the separate brown and white sheets with this page. An adult can
secure them. A drawing of a token does not show this skill. Adult: record coin,
date, help, result, or not assessed.

PRIMARY ONE / WORKBOOK
PRETEND COINS AND CHANGE / WORKBOOK PAGE 7
Fresh genuine-coin checks
Read together. Write or draw in the spaces.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Only when verified examples are ready: the adult changes their
order. Point to each genuine coin or verified official image. Say its
value by yourself. Distinguish it from a pretend card.
Only with genuine coin, pencil and fresh brown and white paper:
make one gentle surface rubbing through each paper. Say the coin
value. Keep both sheets here. The adult supervises and records
results without teaching prompts.
