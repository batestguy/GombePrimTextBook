A solid takes up space. We can turn and hold a safe solid model. A
face here means a flat outside part. On a box model, an edge is
where two flat faces meet.
Mrs Binta turns a box and touches each flat face. She looks straight
at one face, not at a slant. A square has four equal straight sides and
four square corners. A rectangle has four square corners; opposite
sides match. A square is also a rectangle.

P1-17-MODEL / model
Teacher names and identifies cube, non-cube cuboid, cylinder and sphere,
sorts each by features, compares all-equal cube edges with unequal adjacent
edges on the chosen non-cube cuboid, and links a large cube block, long box,
closed straight-sided container and ball to all four respectively.
P1-17-HANDLING / model
On a level surface a sphere and a cylinder on its curved side can both roll; a
cylinder can stand on a flat end. A sphere has no flat end for stable ordinary
stacking. A box may slide or tumble rather than roll smoothly. Record actual
observations, not a predetermined practical result; handling is not assessed
without models.

Teach by touching and turning safe models, not counting only the faces visible
in a picture. Observe six real faces on each box model. A perspective picture
distorts face shapes. A square is a rectangle; some non-cube cuboids have
square faces. Use the most-specific cube group without denying that cubes are
cuboids.
Cover all facing/visible model or name pages and earlier recorded answers
during unaided naming. Collect ALL names before saying any shape name.
Hide the recorded names before named pointing. Record naming, recognition,
grouping, box comparison and all-four examples separately: unaided,
supported or not assessed. Without models, record picture interpretation only,
not handling.
Misconceptions: rolling means sphere; all boxes are cubes; visible faces are
every face. Intervention: touch and turn each large safe model, compare ends
and edges, then use the help page. Close help for the new recheck. Use the
fresh page for transfer and the delayed page on another day with new models.

Glossary: solid: a shape taking up space; face: a flat outside part here; box
edge: where two flat faces meet; square: four equal sides and square corners;
rectangle: four square corners with opposite sides equal; circular: shaped like a
flat circle; curved: bending, not flat; cube, cuboid, cylinder and sphere: see our
feature models.
