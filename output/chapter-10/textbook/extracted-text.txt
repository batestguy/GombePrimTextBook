Chapter ten / Educational review edition
Glossary: ten: a group of ten ones. One: a single item. Place: a digit's position in a
number. Total/sum: how many altogether. Add (+): join amounts. Equals (=): has
the same value as. Check: use a method to see whether an answer is right.
Historical curriculum mapping: P1-p04-04: add two-digit numbers without
regrouping, total at most 40. This chapter also rehearses P1-p04-05 checking
accuracy, including the totals 23 and 25 deferred from Chapter 9. Core objective,
original Q1-Q6 and complete old-to-new answer map are in canonical
production/p1.json. Single-digit addends are deliberate place-value bridging
practice.
