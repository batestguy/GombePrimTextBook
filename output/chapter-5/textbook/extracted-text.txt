Teach, notice and return
Adult guidance / Use several short sessions. Read instructions aloud.
Chapter five / Educational review edition
Progression. TB1 purpose; TB2 ten ones, bundle/unbundle without changing quantity;
TB3 tens on left/ones on right; TB4-5 gradual building/naming of every number 11-20;
TB6 live formation/reading; TB7-8 source tasks; TB9 recall/support; TB10 actual packing
and fresh checks. Do not rush a ladder page as one lesson. Teach each number with
real materials before proceeding.
Prepare. Large paper strips, loose bands, reachable trays/box, pencil/paper and
ordinary cards 10-20. Keep 20 strips for the story; use extra demonstration strips or
return all story strips before closure. Every tied ten contains exactly 10
visible/countable strips. The band is not one of the counted strips. Open bundles to
verify rather than trusting labels or decorative art.
Observe separately. Does the pupil count each one once, make exactly ten, preserve
quantity on tying/untying, read shuffled numerals, place digits correctly and explain
zero loose ones in 20? One bundle is one ten, not one one. The left digit counts tens,
the right counts loose ones. For 10 versus 1, compare actual ten strips with one strip.
Accept usual communication and established adapted writing methods.
Help and fresh recheck. Count 9 then one more, recount 10, tie and open. Privately
prepare a different set of 11 or 13 strips. Ask for complete tens and loose ones, then a
record: 11 gives 1 ten/1 one; 13 gives 1 ten/3 ones. Give no answer before the attempt.
For handwriting, model one two-digit numeral, cover all models and prior answers, then
dictate a different one on fresh space.
Delayed recall. Next session build 18 (1 ten and 8 ones) and 10 (1 ten and 0 ones). Read
shuffled 19, 12, 20, 11. Cover cards and every prior written/printed numeral, then dictate
fifteen, twenty, eleven: 15, 20, 11. Record distinct skill results and revisit difficulties.

Source questions. P1-05-Q1 (TB7/WB2): 12. Q2 (same): 1 ten and 6 ones. Q3 (same):
10 ones. Q4 (TB8/WB4): 19. Q5 (same): 2 bundles, exactly 10 ones in each; 20 total. Q6

pointing/explanation is acceptable.
All models/practice. TB2: 10 loose becomes one ten, still 10 after untying. TB3: 14 is
1 ten and 4 ones; guided 13 is 1 ten and 3 ones. TB4: 11-15 have 1 ten and 1-5 loose
ones respectively. TB5: 16-19 have 1 ten and 6-9 ones respectively; 20 has 2 tens and 0
ones. TB6 cards name eighteen, eleven, twenty, sixteen. WB1: 10 ones; two tens and
no loose ones make 20. WB3: form 10, 14, 20. Digits must be recognisable and in order.
Recall/choice/support/ending. TB9/WB5: open/count 10 once each. Any chosen
11-19 is valid: 1 ten and the remaining 1-9 ones; written numeral, real strips and
recorded digits must agree. Support/recheck is described on TB11. TB10 ending uses
the original 20 markers: exactly 10 in each of 2 bundles, no loose ones. Actually
pack/check before closure.
Fresh checks (TB10/WB6). Privately ask pupil to build 17 and 20: 1 ten/7 ones and 2
tens/0 ones; open/recount to check. Shuffle ALL cards 10-20 for independent naming
without supplied names. Test recognition separately by naming 10, 16, 20 in a different
order. Before dictation cover ALL cards, printed numerals and earlier handwritten
answers, including WB6 build records. Dictate nineteen, ten, fourteen, twenty: 19, 10,
14, 20. Do not show this rubric while testing.
