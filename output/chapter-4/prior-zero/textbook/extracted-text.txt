Teach, notice and return
Adult guidance / Read instructions aloud. Observe separate skills.
Chapter three / Educational review edition
Sequence. Pupil textbook pages 1-6: classroom purpose; two-to-none demonstration
and the meaning of 0; guided one-to-none, recognition, numeral formation and
empty-box drawing; independent source tasks; recall/support; actual packing resolution
and fresh checks. Use the existing book introductions; this continuation does not repeat
them.
Prepare. Large safe counters, reachable tray/mat, storage box, pencil/paper and
ordinary cards 0-5. Demonstrate each physical action. The trays/boxes in exact
diagrams are containers, not counted tops. Never count decorative classroom artwork.
No subtraction or equality notation is needed in these pupil pages.
Notice. Separate empty-set meaning, recognition, independently naming a numeral,
and writing without copying. A child may count the tray as one: ask what is being
counted, then inspect its contents. An open or reversed loop needs formation practice,
not a judgement that the child cannot understand none. Accept usual communication
for naming; use an established adapted writing method where needed.
Intervene, then recheck. Use one counter and another container. Model moving it
away and checking the contents. Link none to 0. Recheck independently with two
counters and a fresh tray, both put away: zero remain. For writing, air-write and trace a
large closed loop, cover the model, then dictate zero on blank paper. Give specific
feedback and retry later.
Delayed recall. Next session put three counters in a different basket, then ask the pupil to
put all away: zero remain. Mix cards 2,0,4: independently name two, zero, four without an
adult supplying the names. Cover them and dictate zero: write 0. Record meaning,
recognition, naming and formation separately; give further supported practice if needed.

Recall/support/ending (TB5-6/WB3). Recall: no objects inside. Support: one then
two counters moved away, zero remain. Ending: zero on mat, three stored; actually
move counters and check the mat before closure. Count stored objects once each; no
objects are lost or added.
Fresh checks (TB6/WB4). Privately prepare trays with 0,2,4 safe counters; change
positions. Empty tray contains 0. Write 0 without copying. Shuffle cards 0-5 (no fixed
order); ask recognition by naming a card, then independently test reading by pointing
without saying its name, including 0 and two other values. Cover cards and dictate zero.
WB4 records separate recognition/reading/writing results; repeated tasks on two
surfaces are one assessment, not two.
Do not prompt an answer before a naming check. Vary tray positions and card order. Compare
observable actions with the rubric; these activities have not been administered to pupils.

the number for none. Numeral: a written number symbol. Mat/tray/box: containers
