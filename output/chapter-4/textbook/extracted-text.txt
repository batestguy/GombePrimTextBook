Teach, notice and return
Adult guidance / Several short sessions, not a race through four numerals.
Chapter four / Educational review edition
Sequence. TB1 purpose; TB2-3 quantities six/seven then eight/nine, numeral names
and one-to-one counting; TB4 live formation/air-writing; TB5 actual sorting; TB6
modelled quantity order and source tasks; TB7 drawing model, recall/support; TB8
actual sorting/counting/ordering resolution, then fresh checks. Do not advance before
each quantity is secure. Existing introductions remain in the opening book.
Prepare. Reachable mats, large safe counters, paper/pencil and cards 1-9. For S002
prepare 6 blue circles, 7 red squares, 8 green triangles and 9 yellow non-square
rectangles. Model sorting by outline; shape naming is not assessed. Printed pieces use
one colour to show the outline rule. All 30 pieces stay in the same four story groups.
Squares remain a special kind of rectangle; use the most specific names here.
Separate skills. Observe quantity, count-once method, recognition, independent
naming, dictation/formation and order separately. Counting correctly does not prove
reading; copying does not prove writing from dictation. For reversed 6 or 9, keep paper
upright and watch the loop/downstroke. Accept usual communication and established
adapted writing methods.
Support/recheck. Use counted/uncounted mats and five counters plus one. Model six,
then independently check a new seven set. If sorting is hard, sort two small groups then
rebuild the target groups. For numeral difficulty, air-write/trace one form, cover it, then
dictate it; later retry a different order. Do not label a child as the weak counter.
Delayed recall, next session. Count fresh sets of 6, 7 and 8. Read shuffled cards 9, 6, 8, 7
independently. Dictate nine, seven, six, eight: 9, 7, 6, 8. Order 8, 6, 9: 6, 8, 9. Sort 7 tops and 9
leaves; state each count. Record which skills need support.

Source tasks. P1-04-Q1 (TB6/WB2): 7. Q2 (same): 8. Q3 (same): 6, 7, 8, 9. Q4
(TB7/WB3): exactly 6 separate dots, any clear arrangement. Q5 (same): exactly 9 real
objects, each counted once; state 9. Q6 (TB7/WB4): recount 8 once per object; the last
word is eight. Moving each counted object is valid. Check the method as well as the
total.
Every model. TB2: 6 is five and one; 7 is five and two. TB3: 8 is five and three; 9 is five
and four. Spreading changes no quantity. TB4/WB1: recognisable 6, 7, 8, 9; loop below
for 6, above for 9. TB5: 6 round/7 square; guided 8 triangle/9 rectangle. TB6: pairing 6
and 7 leaves one unmatched in 7; seven goes between six and eight. Before/after mean
earlier/later in counting order. TB7: 2 counters/2 dots; support 6, recheck 7. TB8: groups
of 6, 7, 8, 9 in increasing row order.
S002 (TB5/WB5). Mix all 30 pieces. Sort by outline, count and record: 6 circles, 7
squares, 8 triangles, 9 non-square rectangles. Order: 6, 7, 8, 9. Observe actual sorting;
written counts alone do not demonstrate it. For story closure (TB8), verify all 30 pieces
in these groups before starting the pretend game.
Fresh checks (TB8/WB6). Privately prepare new scattered groups of 8 and 9; answers
8 and 9. S003: count 9 objects once each, then independently name ALL shuffled cards
1-9 without supplied names. Test recognition separately by naming 6, 7, 8, 9 in a
different order. Before dictation, cover ALL cards, printed numerals and the pupil's
handwritten count answers, including the totals on WB6. S004: dictate eight, six, nine,
seven; answers 8, 6, 9, 7, correctly oriented. Do not show answers or read this rubric
aloud while testing.
Same tasks on both surfaces count as one check. New physical objects, independently shuffled
naming and covered-model dictation are fresh evidence. No tasks have been administered to
pupils.

Words. Six/seven/eight/nine: quantities after five. Count: say one number word per
object. Numeral: written number symbol. Sort: group by a rule. Order: arrange from
fewest to most here. Before/after: earlier/later in the counting sequence. Outline: the
edge of a shape. Shape names may be read by an adult; they are sorting materials, not
a new naming test.

-03: count/read through 9; -04: write 6-9; -05: quantity order. Original P1-04-Q1-Q6
prompts, answers and order retained. S002 adds actual four-type sorting; S003 adds
shuffled reading; S004 adds independent dictation. The historical baseline and
suggested pacing do not establish current curriculum alignment.
