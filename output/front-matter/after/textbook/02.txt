Read with an adult
Ask an adult to read the words and help you find a page. Take turns. Ask
questions. Everyone can join in.
Use your textbook
Keep this textbook for reading and sharing. Say, point or use objects. Write
and draw in your workbook or an ordinary exercise book.
Find a lesson
Look at the contents on the next pages. The number beside a title tells you
the page in THIS textbook. An adult can help you read the page numbers.
Try in your own way
You can speak, point, move objects or draw. Use safe materials within easy
reach. Take time to explain your thinking.
