Teach, observe and revisit
Read together. Adult guidance and answers.
Chapter eight / Educational review edition
Suggested sessions: TB1-2 concrete joining; TB3 signs and equality; TB4
three sets; TB5 drawing; TB6 / WB2-3 independent tasks; TB7 help; TB8 and
WB6 fresh checks. This is flexible editorial pacing, not an official timetable.
Read directions aloud. Use large safe counters or paper circles. Keep all
materials reachable for seated participation. Allow touch, pointing, speech
and drawing. Model the move into one row; do not introduce counting-on,
carrying or totals through eighteen here.
Observe: Does the child preserve all objects, count each once, distinguish
the separate sets from the total and read the entire number sentence? If a
child counts a set twice, return to 1+1, moving each counter once into one
row. Then use the new 2+1 recheck (TB7 / WB4). If equals is treated as an
answer arrow, build 3 and 2+1 side by side and read 3=2+1.
Delayed recall, next session: Without showing earlier answers, ask the
child to join two and two counters and record the total. Then join one, one
and two. Ask whether 4=2+2 is true and why. Answers: 4; 4; yes, both sides
have value 4. Record whether the child worked independently or with
support.
Do not infer mastery from copied numerals. Return to concrete objects when
needed.
