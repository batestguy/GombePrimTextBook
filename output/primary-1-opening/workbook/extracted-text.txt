PRIMARY ONE / WORKBOOK
SORT, MATCH AND ORDER / TRY TOGETHER
Sort and match
Watch Mr Aliyu first. You may mark this workbook.
Read together. Write in this workbook.
Mr Aliyu circled one leaf. Circle the other leaf.
Leave the tops without circles.
Mix real leaves and tops at the table. Sort them into two groups. Count
each group. Tell your partner the rule.
Draw one line from each top in the upper row to a different top below.
Does every top have a partner?
Tell your partner: more, fewer, or the same number?
