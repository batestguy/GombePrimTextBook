# Chapter handoff

Mandatory references: [CHAPTER-STANDARD.md](CHAPTER-STANDARD.md), version 1, and
[CHAPTER-REVIEW.md](CHAPTER-REVIEW.md). Copy these references into every handoff.

- Objective, chapter ID, intended pupils and acceptance criteria:
- Author; assigned independent reviewer/session (or pending):
- Canonical objective/question IDs; overlay, renderer and artwork paths:
- Current PDF and rendered-page evidence; build/validation commands and results
  (for Primary 1, identify BOTH textbook and workbook, their cross-links and the
  respective S1-S9 pages; a single artifact does not approve the pair):
- S1–S9 page map; purposeful variations with reasons:
- Lead teacher, child roles, preferences and cross-chapter continuity checks:
- Opening page: lead teacher at the blackboard, four seated children facing the teacher, exact topic-hint wording and rendered-page legibility:
- Every-task mathematics/diagram/answer check coverage:
- Instruction, prerequisite and story-logic findings:
- Fingerprint and review gate result; unresolved blocking/required findings:
- Approvals reset to pending before source edits/rebuild; shared approvals affected:
- Local curriculum, educator, classroom, accessibility and print evidence/pending:
- Next concrete action, owned files, constraints and known risks:

Preserve `GombeTextbook.txt` byte for byte and all user changes. Keep credentials
outside the repository. A receiving reviewer reports scope and current evidence
before reviewing. An executor must not overwrite another contributor's changes.
Run preflight before a session handoff. Never convert pending checks into approval.
