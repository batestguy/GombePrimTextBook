## 1. Annual scope and planning assumptions

Primary 1 is a full academic year organised into **three terms**, not nineteen
weeks or one term. The existing **19 units are topic containers**. Each expands
into multiple teaching steps, practice sessions, practical activities and checks.
Their source IDs are not a timetable or a required final chapter count.

Use **36 school weeks** as a flexible production-planning allowance: 12 per term,
comprising approximately 10 teaching weeks, one revision/support week and one
assessment/contingency week. This is an editorial approximation, not a verified
Gombe calendar. Schools may redistribute time for holidays, local calendars,
starting levels and re-teaching. Treat a teaching week as several mathematics
sessions, not one lesson. For workload estimation only, four sessions per teaching
week yields about 120 teaching sessions across the year; this is not an official
period allocation or a demand to fill every session with new content.

Spiral earlier learning throughout the year. Count, read, explain and check
small quantities regularly while introducing measurement, shapes and data in
meaningful contexts. Read pupil instructions aloud. Allow children to point,
speak, move objects or draw before requiring written responses.

## 6. Assessment and completion rules

For every baseline objective, record its taught model, supported practice,
independent task, answer/rubric, smaller-step intervention, fresh recheck and delayed
recall where relevant. Distinguish a direct question sample from comprehensive
teaching and distinguish opportunity from observed pupil mastery.

Use three term revision checkpoints, term assessment opportunities and a whole-year
check. Existing three revision/two final-assessment sets provide 30 draft items
to adapt; author missing term-specific tasks rather than relabelling an existing
set without inspection. Use oral/practical/written responses appropriate to each
skill. Do not turn a shape-handling or collection objective into a reading test.

Three completion states must remain distinct:

1. **Editorially complete under this provisional plan:** every adopted objective
   and enrichment is taught/practised/checked, every task has an answer, both outputs
   assemble correctly, and the entire year plus front/back matter is present.
2. **Independently reviewed:** all S1-S9 evidence, mathematics, instructions,
   continuity, story logic, answers, cross-book links and every rendered page pass
   a distinct reviewer with fresh fingerprints. Resource-conditional claims stay
   conditional; do not mark missing practical evidence completed.
3. **Verified for adoption/printing:** applicable current curriculum and state
   requirements reconciled; local educator/pupil evidence, actual-size print proof
   and printer requirements genuinely checked. These checks may remain pending
   while drafting and editorial review proceed.
