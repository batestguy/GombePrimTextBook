## Educational and local review

- Are instructions short enough when read aloud? Which words need replacement or extra oral modelling?
- Do the stories, clothing, materials and pictures fit varied Gombe communities without implying a single way of life?
- Are number ranges, concrete models and question difficulty suitable for the intended class?
- Can pupils explain their reasoning? Which lesson needs another example or a smaller step?
- Do seated, low-vision and other pupils have practical routes to participate?
- Which adopted curriculum edition and transition cohort did the reviewer verify? Attach its official source/identifier.
- Which genuine coin/note examples were verified? Record authoritative source, date and denominations; distinguish historical specimens.
