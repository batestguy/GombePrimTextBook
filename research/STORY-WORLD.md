# Proposed story world and evidence boundaries

Current authority: [CHAPTER-STANDARD.md](../docs/CHAPTER-STANDARD.md) and [independent review](../docs/CHAPTER-REVIEW.md). Active character/teacher registries supersede historical proposals below. Edit those registries in place; the historical create_storyboard.py seed is disabled when current registries exist. All subsequent handoffs use docs/HANDOFF-TEMPLATE.md.

Status: proposed for prototype review, 17 October 2026. Character reference and prototype approval remain pending. This registry does not authorise generation of the remaining scenes.

The same four friends grow from Primary 1 to Primary 3. Aishatu has family roots in Gombe, Adamu in Dukku, Grace in Billiri and John in Yamaltu-Deba. Their personalities, recoverable mistakes and strengths are specified in `production/characters.json`. None of those fictional roots establishes ethnicity, religion, language or a typical household. John uses a manual wheelchair, participates actively and has reachable work surfaces and clear approaches. He is an organiser, learner and friend as often as the others.

Primary 1 establishes the club, makes paper toys and gathers a discovery album. Primary 2 builds a fictional seed library, hosts an activity day and designs a reading corner. Primary 3 tests community games, plans a school garden and shares a story-and-data exhibition. The notebook, album, reused craft materials and family relationships carry forward. Family visits, printed messages, letters and albums connect places without implying that the class takes frequent long trips. Scene changes do not establish real routes, travel times or actual named institutions.

`production/storyboard.json` contains nine arc openings and 63 individually authored lesson situations: 19, 20 and 24 for Primary 1, 2 and 3. The existing lesson ranges supply editorial term pacing. Mathematical objectives are copied exactly from canonical data. The registry supplies proposed pupil Look text and illustration direction; it does not modify canonical questions or answers. All four children lead in every grade, and every child is assigned each of the initiating, struggling, explaining and helping roles. Roles direct later dialogue and are not claims that all four actions already appear in each short Look paragraph.

All eleven LGAs appear in at least five proposed lesson situations. Each setting has a practical problem, family connection or club purpose. `research/story-coverage.csv` records each scene and its administrative fact reference. Named households, workshops, schools, quantities and events are fiction. No scene claims to document a real heritage practice. A substantive editorial audit remains pending: integrate the agreed personalities and recurring guests throughout the narratives, reconcile role assignments with enacted actions, and assess whether each family connection achieves sufficient local specificity. Structural counts alone do not establish acceptance of the full rewrite.

The official [Ministry LGA budget index](https://www.mof.gm.gov.ng/approved-gombe-lgas-approved-2025-budgets/) provides the administrative list; the search-index text was accessible, while the direct page timed out during verification. The [state development-plan listing](https://www.mof.gm.gov.ng/download/gombe-state-development-plan-2/) is a research lead, and its full document has not been audited. Distinguish the 2020-2030 abridged listing from the 2021-2030 listing before citing a specific edition.

The supplied [Forebears Gombe page](https://forebears.io/nigeria/gombe/forenames) returned HTTP 403. The accessible [Forebears methodology](https://forebears.io/about/name-distribution-and-demographics) describes estimates and uneven samples. It cannot establish that these four names represent present-day Gombe pupils or identify their communities. No ranking or frequency is asserted.

The [Tangale heritage project](https://heritagemanagement.org/projects/p2/tangale-cultural-heritage/) documents language-preservation work associated with Billiri, Kaltungo and Shongom. This does not validate an invented costume, motif, food, festival or migration narrative. Community consultation and local educational review remain pending before any specific cultural claim is introduced.

Generated illustrations show relationships and practical settings. Exact assessed counts, fractions, clocks, money values, measurements and graph marks must be drawn deterministically and checked against answers. Teaching quantities are fictional and must never be presented as measured local demographic, agricultural or economic data. No current NERDC alignment, physical print testing, local educational approval, printer approval or press-ready export is claimed.

Rebuild the registries with `python scripts/create_storyboard.py`. The script checks lesson counts, unique lesson scene IDs, geography coverage, all four grade-level leads and role coverage. Canonical lesson files are inputs only. Review new story wording with the prototype before integrating it into the renderer.
