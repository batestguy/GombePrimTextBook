Question: is an alphabetical index necessary in the companion Primary 1 workbook?

Scope: small publisher/platform example check, not a representative market survey or an official curriculum requirement. No book content copied.

Evidence checked:
- Collins International Primary Maths Workbook 1 (ISBN9780008457181), publisher-branded digital edition on BlinkLearning: visible navigation lists intro, contents, how to use and topic units. This establishes use of contents/unit navigation; the preview alone does not prove absence of every possible back-matter index. https://marsupial.blinklearning.com/coursePlayer/curso2.php?idcurso=4018654

It does not establish a universal requirement for an alphabetical index. https://oup.com.pk/school-textbooks/mathematics/new-syllabus-primary-mathematics-2nd-edition.html
- Collins Primary Maths for Scotland1A PracticeWorkbook1: publisher describes additional practice/consolidation and use alongside a textbook or independently. The product description does not establish whether its full print edition has an index. https://collins.co.uk/products/9780008680275
- Cambridge stage1 front-matter sample requests were inaccessible through the research tool; no conclusion about their index contents was drawn.

Editorial inference: an alphabetical index is optional for this early-primary workbook. Designed contents and clear activity locations are sufficient navigation for the companion practice book; an alphabetical concept index is useful in the reusable textbook.

Owner decision: explicit reply on18November2026 selected workbook contents only, textbook contents and index. Both books retain the approved author biography as their last page. No claim of a universal publishing rule or current curriculum alignment.
