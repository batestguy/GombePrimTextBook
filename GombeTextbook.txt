A Practical Guide to Sourcing, Referencing, and Licensing Images
Using images responsibly is critical. Here is a step-by-step guide:

Source Your Images: Use the sources listed above. Always prefer open-license or public domain images to avoid copyright issues.

Check the License: Before using any image, verify its license.

Public Domain: No restrictions.

Creative Commons (CC): You must follow the specific terms (e.g., CC BY requires attribution; CC BY-SA requires you to share your book under the same license).

Copyrighted: You must get explicit permission from the copyright holder.

Reference Your Images: This is essential for academic integrity and for your book to be credible. Nigerian educational institutions have standard citation formats. Use a clear, consistent style. For an image in your textbook, a reference should include:

Creator's Name (if known)

Year of Creation (if known)

Title or Description of the Image

Source (e.g., Museum Name, Website, Book Title)

License (e.g., CC BY-SA 4.0)

🤖 Integrating AI Skills: A Progressive Approach
Integrating AI literacy is a forward-thinking move that aligns with the Gombe State government's focus on digital literacy. The goal is to build AI literacy: the knowledge, skills, and attitudes to understand and interact with AI responsibly. You can structure this using the AI Literacy (AILit) Framework: Engage with, Create with, Manage, and Shape AI.

For Book 1 (Primary 1-3): "Discovering AI"

Engage with AI: Introduce the concept of "smart" machines. Activity: Sort pictures of Gombe crops into "maize" and "millet" piles, mimicking how AI learns.

Create with AI: Use a simple, block-based coding app (like ScratchJr) to create a story about a character from Gombe.

Manage AI: A simple discussion about asking an adult before using smart devices.

For Book 2 (Primary 4-6): "Understanding and Using AI"

Engage with AI: Explain how AI learns from data. Project: Collect data on rainfall in different Gombe LGAs and create a chart to discuss how AI might predict weather.

Create with AI: Use tools like LearningML to train a model to recognize images of local landmarks.

Manage AI: Discuss data privacy and asking critical questions ("Is this information correct?").

Shape AI: Discuss how AI can be designed to help solve problems important to Gombe, such as improving farming techniques.
